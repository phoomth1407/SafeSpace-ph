✦

☏

Find support

Browse hotline, emergency-contact, and wellbeing reading information when you need another source of support.

◎

Read public Community posts and comments. Signed-in users can join in with a username or anonymous label.

Privacy by design

Your birthdate stays private.

For account assessments, SafeSpace checks age from a private profile record. The full birthdate is stored separately from public and admin-readable profile data; it is not shown in Community or the admin user list.

Sharing risk score and risk level with administrators is a separate choice. Assessment answers and written responses are not part of that shared score view.

How it is built

A web app with a protected backend.

The interface uses React and Vite and is hosted on GitHub Pages. Supabase provides sign-in, the database, realtime Community updates, and server-side functions. AI provider keys stay on the server.

Signed-in wellbeing answers may be processed by configured AI providers. The main wellbeing record saves score/category metadata; PHQ-9 uses a separate flow that saves its answers and result fields.

A clear boundary

Screening and support, not diagnosis.

SafeSpace is an independent school project. It is not a clinic, therapist, emergency service, or clinical monitoring system. AI can misunderstand context, Community posts are user-generated, and online services may fail. For urgent help, contact local emergency services or a trusted person.
