# SafeSpace Assessment Participation & Privacy Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last updated:** 19 September 2026

This policy explains what happens when a person uses the SafeSpace wellbeing assessment. SafeSpace is a school-project wellbeing screening and support application. It is not a medical provider, diagnostic service, emergency service, or substitute for professional care.

## 1. Purpose and scope
The assessment is intended to help users reflect on wellbeing-related patterns and receive supportive explanations and suggested next steps. Participation is voluntary.

## 2. Information collected
The assessment may process assessment answers, age, selected nationality, language preference, generated assessment results, and account information required for authenticated sessions. The assessment does not ask for a real name, home address, or precise location. Users should not place unnecessary identifying information into free-text responses.

## 3. How information is used
Assessment information is used to generate results, provide supportive explanations and recommendations, maintain authenticated assessment history, operate the service, improve reliability, and protect against abuse.

## 4. AI processing
Some submitted answers may be processed by third-party AI services. The current main assessment function can use OpenAI and Gemini as configured fallbacks, with local fallback logic when remote AI is unavailable. AI output can be inaccurate and is not a diagnosis.

## 5. Guest and authenticated storage
Authenticated assessment results may be stored in the user's SafeSpace account and shown in assessment history. Guest results are intended to remain in the current browser session and are not saved to the SafeSpace account.

## 6. Community content
Community posts are separate from private assessment history. Content deliberately published to Community may be visible to other users and may be moderated. Do not publish passwords, private contact details, sensitive personal information, or another person's personal information.

## 7. Security
SafeSpace uses Supabase authentication, database Row Level Security, request validation, rate limits, and other safeguards. No internet service can guarantee complete security.

## 8. Children and teenagers
SafeSpace may be used by teenagers. It is not professional care. Users should involve a parent, guardian, teacher, counselor, or another trusted adult when appropriate, particularly when professional help is needed.

## 9. Retention and deletion
Authenticated assessment records may remain in the account until deleted through available account features or an authorized administrative process. Guest results are not intentionally stored as account records. As a school project, SafeSpace may have more limited retention/deletion controls than a commercial clinical platform.

## 10. Third-party services
SafeSpace uses third-party infrastructure and services including Supabase, GitHub Pages, Google sign-in when selected, and AI providers when AI analysis is enabled. Each provider has its own terms and privacy practices.

## 11. Policy changes
This policy may be updated when assessment behavior, data handling, security controls, or third-party services change. Material changes should be reflected in the published policy and may require renewed review before a future assessment.

## 12. Emergencies
SafeSpace is not an emergency service and cannot monitor users continuously. In an immediate emergency, contact an appropriate local emergency service or a trusted adult. In Thailand, the Department of Mental Health hotline is **1323**.

## 13. Acceptance
By selecting **Agree & Accept** after reaching the end of this policy, the user confirms that they have read and understood it and voluntarily agree to the collection, use, storage, and AI processing described above. The user can close the policy instead and leave without submitting assessment answers.
