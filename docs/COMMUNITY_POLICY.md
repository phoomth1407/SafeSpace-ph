# SafeSpace Community Policy

Last reviewed: 30 September 2026

The full bilingual Community Policy is maintained in the application's policy source and rendered by the Community policy flow. This repository document summarizes the current scope so contributors can find the relevant rules without treating this file as a separate policy implementation.

## Core rules

The Community is intended for respectful peer discussion and wellbeing-related sharing. Users should:

- respect other people and their differences
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, humiliation, or targeted abuse
- protect their own privacy and other people's private information
- avoid presenting personal experiences as professional medical advice
- avoid content that is inappropriate for a youth-oriented wellbeing community
- avoid spam, scams, impersonation, misleading claims, or disruptive behavior
- share only content they have the right to share
- use reporting and moderation tools responsibly

## Youth and privacy

SafeSpace is designed with younger users in mind. Users should not request or expose private contact details, home/school locations, private images, or other identifying information. An anonymous display name is not a guarantee of complete anonymity because technical systems can still process information needed to operate and protect the service.

## Posting and commenting

Community participation is authenticated. Users must acknowledge the Community Policy when the application requires it before posting or commenting. Normal accounts are subject to the application's posting protections; administrative/test accounts can have separate moderation privileges. The current system also has database-side protection, so a client-side timer is not the only enforcement layer.

## Moderation

Content may be reviewed after reports, when it appears to violate policy, or when safety/abuse concerns require review. Depending on circumstances, SafeSpace may leave content in place, request changes, remove content, limit Community features, or restrict an account.

## Support and emergencies

Community discussion is not an emergency service or professional treatment. In Thailand, the application provides Mental Health Hotline 1323 information. Immediate emergencies should be directed to an appropriate emergency service rather than waiting for a Community response.

## Source of truth

The user-facing policy should be kept synchronized with the Community implementation. When changing Community posting, comments, reports, moderation, or policy acknowledgement, update this document and review the relevant policy source and database controls.
